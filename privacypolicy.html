Privacy, Safety & Platform Policy

Privacy, Safety & Platform Policy · Effective September 2, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how MineIQ may process information across Surface Mining, Underground Mining, MineIQ Jobs, DGMS Updates, account services, reports, calculators, records, exports, Mine Manager Simulator and AI-assisted features where available.

2. Information MineIQ May Process

Depending on the features you use, this may include account/profile details, email address and authentication identifiers; mine/company information; production, development, machinery, fuel, dispatch, inspection, accident/near-miss, drilling/blasting, explosive and operational records; underground shift, ventilation, gas/atmosphere and ground-control information; calculations, notes and reports; locally saved preferences; and technical/diagnostic information where supported.

MineIQ Jobs may process vacancy details and, for employer/recruiter submissions, company/mine details, recruiter name/role, contact email, optional contact phone, application links, authorization declarations and account identifiers associated with the submission.

If an AI feature is enabled, information entered into the AI feature may be processed by the relevant AI service provider to generate a response.

3. Use of Information

  • Operate, secure and maintain MineIQ.
  • Authenticate supported accounts.
  • Save, retrieve and where supported synchronize records.
  • Generate calculations, reports and exports requested by users.
  • Operate and moderate MineIQ Jobs submissions.
  • Display and organize DGMS-related public information and official-source links.
  • Diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse and improve reliability.

MineIQ does not intend to sell users' personal information.

4. Local Storage, Offline Use & Cloud

MineIQ uses local device storage for supported offline records, preferences, saved items and Mine Manager Simulator progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling the application may remove local information. Supported signed-in features may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Guest mode may not provide the same synchronization or recovery capabilities as signed-in use.

5. Third-Party Services & Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide information necessary to authenticate your account, such as your email address and permitted basic profile information. MineIQ may use third-party providers for cloud functionality, authentication, diagnostics, security or enabled AI functionality. Their processing is also governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

6. MineIQ Jobs

MineIQ Jobs is a job-discovery and information feature. MineIQ is not the employer or recruiting authority unless a listing expressly states otherwise. A listing, review or "verified" label does not guarantee that the vacancy remains open, that all details are error-free, that an applicant is eligible, or that employment, selection, salary or another outcome will result.

Always verify the vacancy, eligibility, dates, fees (if any), employer identity and application instructions through the employer's or recruiting authority's official source. Never send money, passwords, OTPs or sensitive financial information to an unverified recruiter. MineIQ moderation of a submitted vacancy is not a legal, financial, background or employment guarantee.

7. DGMS Updates & Government Information

MineIQ is independent and is not the Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS), the Government of India or another government authority, and must not be understood as endorsed, approved or operated by them unless an official authority expressly states so.

DGMS Updates is a convenience layer for discovering and organizing public information. MineIQ summaries, alerts, extracted points or dates may be incomplete, delayed, reformatted or contain errors. The complete material published by the competent authority remains the authoritative source. Verify the current official source before acting on regulatory, examination, certification, eligibility, compliance or safety information.

8. Mining & Safety-Critical Use

MineIQ calculations, checklists, shift tools, records, dashboards and reports are productivity aids. They are not statutory certifications, permits, statutory registers, engineering designs, safety clearances or automatic approvals to commence or continue work.

For blasting/explosives, ventilation, gas/atmosphere, ground control, machinery, geotechnical, electrical, emergency, transport or other safety-critical matters, follow applicable law, mine-specific approved procedures, statutory requirements, manufacturer instructions, risk assessments and the decisions of authorized/competent persons. If MineIQ information conflicts with an official requirement or authorized site instruction, the official requirement or instruction takes priority.

9. AI-Assisted Features

AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated. It is informational/productivity support only and must not be treated as legal or regulatory advice, statutory interpretation, engineering certification, blast authorization, safety clearance, emergency instruction or a substitute for a qualified/competent person's judgment. Do not enter passwords, OTPs, payment-card information or information you are not authorized to disclose.

10. Reports, PDF/Excel Exports & Sharing

Generation by MineIQ does not by itself make a report a statutory record, certified document, legally approved return or official submission. Users are responsible for checking accuracy, completeness, signatures/approvals, required formats and statutory obligations. Files shared to WhatsApp, email, cloud storage or another service are then handled outside MineIQ and may be subject to that service's privacy practices.

11. Data Sharing & Security

Information may be processed or disclosed as reasonably necessary to provide enabled services, operate authentication/cloud infrastructure, process user-initiated AI requests, provide support, maintain security, prevent abuse, comply with applicable law or lawful requests, or protect the rights and safety of MineIQ and its users. MineIQ uses reasonable security measures, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Retention, Deletion & User Choices

Information may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide functionality, maintain accounts, support security, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Depending on the feature, users may edit or remove local records, export supported data, sign out, manage device permissions or request deletion of eligible account-associated information. Identity verification may be required, and limited information may be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

13. Children

MineIQ is designed for mining professionals, trainees, students, employers and industry users, not as a children's service. Anyone who cannot legally provide required consent under applicable law should use MineIQ only with the involvement or authorization required by law.

14. No Professional, Legal or Regulatory Advice

Nothing in MineIQ is legal advice, regulatory advice or a representation that a particular activity complies with law. Requirements can change and can depend on mine type, location, approvals, licences, roles, equipment and circumstances. Consult current official requirements and appropriate qualified professionals or competent authorities when needed.

15. Changes & Contact

MineIQ may update this policy as the app, data practices, third-party services or applicable requirements change. For privacy questions, support or eligible account/data deletion requests, contact officialmineiq@gmail.com.

16. Acknowledgement
