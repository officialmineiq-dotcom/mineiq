Burden & Spacing Estimator

Uses a simplified rule-of-thumb (Burden ≈ 25–30× hole diameter × rock factor). Verify against your actual blast design standards before field use.

Charge & Stemming Estimator

Uses rule-of-thumb: Stemming ≈ 0.7 × Burden; Linear Charge (kg/m) = 0.785 × (dia in m)² × SG × 1000. Verify against your certified blast design before field use.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Welcome to MineIQ ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how MineIQ collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, MineIQ may collect the following information:

Account Information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile information (if provided)

Mine Data:

  • Mine diary entries
  • Equipment records
  • Inspection checklists
  • Generated reports
  • Notes and observations
  • Calculator history (where applicable)

Device Information:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information

AI Assistant Requests: If you use the AI Assistant, your questions and prompts may be processed by third-party AI service providers to generate responses.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the MineIQ application.
  • Save and synchronize your data across devices.
  • Generate reports and documents.
  • Improve app performance and reliability.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Improve AI Assistant functionality.
  • Develop new features.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your information may be stored:

  • On your device.
  • On secure cloud servers if you sign in and enable synchronization.

Reasonable security measures are used to protect your information from unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

MineIQ does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With trusted cloud service providers that help operate the application.
  • With AI service providers when processing AI Assistant requests.
  • When required by law or legal process.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the application.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to help protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • View your stored information.
  • Edit your information.
  • Delete your account.
  • Request deletion of your stored data.
  • Export your information where supported.

7. Offline Usage

Many MineIQ features can be used offline. When synchronization is enabled, locally stored information may be uploaded securely to your account to provide backup and access across multiple devices.

8. AI Assistant

The AI Assistant is designed to provide educational and productivity support for mining professionals. AI-generated responses are provided for informational purposes only and should not replace professional engineering judgment, mine management procedures, or regulatory requirements. Users remain responsible for verifying all technical calculations and operational decisions.

9. Third-Party Services

MineIQ may use third-party services such as:

  • Authentication providers (for example, Google Sign-In)
  • Cloud storage services
  • Analytics services
  • AI service providers
  • Crash reporting services

These providers maintain their own privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

MineIQ is intended for professionals and students in the mining industry. The application is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will become effective when the updated Privacy Policy is published within the application or on our website.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact:

MineIQ Support
Email: officialmineiq@gmail.com

13. Consent

By using MineIQ, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection and use of information as described in this document.
